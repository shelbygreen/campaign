While attempting to locate the email address of my State Representative on the Florida House website, I realized how annoying it is to find basic contact information for elected officials. Emails shouldn't exist bured in PDFs - they should be displayed publicly, so constituents are encouraged to contact their legislators.  
\n
I wrote a script to scrape contact information for each State Legislator from the House website. I shared the data below. This type of information should be open and accessible.  

I hope this makes it easier to contact your Legislator and share your concerns!
