There's a growing community effort to encourage the City of Tallahassee to purchase Holton Apartments. 

Community members have encouraged the City to purchase the 100-unit property to pre-empt outside developers from raising rents and displacement, and further reducing stable, affordable housing units on the market.

This potential municipal venture sparks a lot of questions: 
- who will manage the property, the City or the Tallahassee Housing Authority?
- if it's managed by the City, are there enough human resources to manage a 100-unit property?

While I don't think it's realistic, yet, for the City to fully own and manage this property, the City Commission should absolutely deliberate over possible options, like:
- partial ownership, where one of the buildings can be used for rapid rehousing or subsidized short-term housing
- incentivizing the potential owner to support inclusionary housing, where 20-50% of the units stay below market-rate
