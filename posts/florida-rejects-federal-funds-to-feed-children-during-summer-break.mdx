39 States [accepted federal funds](https://www.fns.usda.gov/summer/sunbucks#usa-map-links) this year to help families feed their children during Summer Break. But 12 Republican-led States, including Florida, chose to reject the funds, denying families a $120-per-child credit to help families afford to buy groceries and feed their children while they're out of school.
