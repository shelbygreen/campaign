Election Day is November 5, 2024. Like every election, this one will shape our future and decide which path we’ll take as Americans, but this year’s election has more severe outcomes than usual. Will we choose to decline further into incivility and unaffordability, or end the chaos and work together to reshape our country into one that is fair, affordable, and safe for all?

Make sure you’re registered to vote so you can help choose trusted leaders to move our communities closer to a better tomorrow, where hope still exists and stability, peace, and prosperity are still in reach.

<b>If you live in Florida, you have until tomorrow, 11:59pm, to register. Register or check your registration online [here](https://www.registertovoteflorida.gov)</b>. 

### Other Key Election Dates
* <b>October 24: Last day to request a vote-by-mail ballot (VBM) for the 2024 General Election</b>. Request instructions [here](https://dos.fl.gov/elections/for-voters/voting/vote-by-mail/).
* <b>October 26 - November 2: Early voting period</b>. Each county Supervisor of Elections may offer more days of early voting. Find your county's early voting schedule [here](https://www.myfloridaelections.com/Contact-your-SOE).
* <b>November 5: Election Day</b>, and last opportunity to vote in the 2024 General Election. Find your polling location [here](https://dos.fl.gov/elections/for-voters/check-your-voter-status-and-polling-place/voter-precinct-lookup/).
