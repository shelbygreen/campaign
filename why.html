Why I'm Running

  1. No one should worry about getting home safely. / Vision Zero / No deaths from traffic accidents (article)
  2. Securing affordable housing starts with preserving and upgrading our existing housing stocks. Developers shouldn't be allowed to bulldoze housing and sit on land until the price is right. It's an invasive practice that has gone on for far too long.
  3. Energy independence / reduce reliance on all forms of gas -- whether it's renewable natural gas, hydrogen gas, or just plain natural gas. We need to invest in innovative energy efficiency, designing buildings that require less electricity, and incorporating solar in building design. Embrace electrification now so we don't have to worry about the future
  4. Our elected officials can't afford to ignore the call to serve, especially in a desperate time of need. We don't deserve to worry if we'll make it home safely, or how to get the lights turned back on. We don’t deserve this treatment. We deserve elected officials that will do everything in their power to serve the public and their needs.
  5. We deserve a future where our lives are treated with respect and care, where re-occuring problems, like car crashes and traffic deaths, are addressed.
  6. The City is not prepared for the unexpected. The City responded to COVID by leaving the County, and other local partners, to respond to call of distress.
  7. Data and policy transparency to increase accountability & make informed decisions & take advantage of opportunities (example: utility disconnections)
  8. We're recovering from a pandemic. Open to ideas and competition by increasing retention from graduating students at TCC, FAMU, and FSU. We have access to an abundance of research (FSU, FAMU, TCC) and social resources (non-profits) that we're letting go to waste.
  9. I'm Shelby Green and I'm running to be Tallahassee's next City Commissioner. As Commissioner, I will prioritize the public in public funds, services, and infrastructure.
