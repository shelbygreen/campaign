i met will this morning to discuss the vision zero map. before we actually worked on it though, we spoke about the comments i made to him about adner being a pawn. in a sense, he is. he only received help after i had announced my candidacy. i wonder if he feels like he's being used or recognizes it. 

part of me felt like i was in the sucken place when i was in the our tallahassee group. it was such a cult. i dont even know if i wrote about it, but on saturday, while i was at donna's birthday party, maggi asked to talk to me. she looked so anxious. she talked about how sorry she was to put an adner sign in my yard and for her public display of support for adner. it's like she was begging for my acceptance. and for her to tell her it's okay and that she hasn't lost her morals or herself. 

i just listened. and then i told her i understand. i've been in her exact position -- doing something out of intense social pressure or following "orders" or tolerating disrespect because going against the grain would lead to social isolation and a form of obsolencense. 

look at me -- im someone who finally said no and have been completed ostracized. 

but anyway, that was a lot to handle. i wanted to write some fragment of that night down while it was on my mind. going back to today...

while talking to will, so much anger rose to the surface. anger that i've buried for a long time. it reminded me though that people in the progressive political circle think of adner as the shoe-in candidate and i need to show them that he's not. by just continuing to share myself, my work, and my passion.
