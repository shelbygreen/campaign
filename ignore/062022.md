8:47pm -- today was supposed to be a chill day, but i'm now realizing that there will be no more chill days until the election. i need to make the most of every day, and the easiest way for me to do that is by canvassing. it's time to turn up the heat!!

i started my day by going to the Courthouse Annex to witness an eviction case. i'm not going to deep dive into the case, because it's all in my notes, but it was interesting being there. it was emotional. a man was evicting a woman, who had been living in his home for over 10 years,  because she couldn't afford to pay the rent increase. she previously split the rent with her mother and partner, but they both passed within the last two years due to long COVID. she was an employee at FSU and didn't have a car. i was just thinking about how life is so unfair for some people and that our political and justice systems do not provide the safety net that they should.

that was the main thing i wanted to get out of my head. i'm going to make a post about it tomorrow but it's just so frustrating because it shouldn't be this way. this woman has 10 days to find a new home and just spent $1,600 in backpay. what kind of home will she be able to find? what happens if she's not able to find a home? there should be some kind of City prescence at these meeting to provide information on how to apply and obtain rapid rehousing. it took Ms. Nicole several months to be able to find housing. the City is failing the people. 

after the eviction case, i went over to Whole Foods to finish my questionnaire for the Run for Something endorsement. i'm glad Monet and I worked on it yesterday. i think i wrote a solid bio and pitch. it may need to be edited. i'll ask Joe to take a look at it. 

after that, i went home, slept, had a meeting with the Citizens for Ethics Reform, and then a call with a fundraising consulting group. the call actually stressed me out because it made me realize how far behind i am in terms of fundraising. i know it'll all work itself out, but i just don't know how yet. the good thing about the stress is i went for a canvassing walk around the neighborhood. it was actually really successful. i think evenings, on week nights, work best. 

i do need to kick it up a gear, but i also need to remember to relax and to take it one step at a time. it'll all work out!
