something that i didn't write about yesterday is there was a homeless man that showed up to our house yesterday. he stopped by shortly after benji and i were dropped off. it was an uncomfortable exchange. jeffrey, the homeless man, is not a stranger. we've seen him a few times before and have offered help when he asked for it. the story that he used is he's waiting to go to the VA hospital. but i don't think he has an appointment at the VA hospital. i've seen how bad his foot is. it's literally eating itself. i gave him what i had, but while talking to him, i felt a small fraction of what it might've been like to a slave owner. he said "you're up there, im down here". im not completely recounting the story, but just adding fragments that stood out to me. 

tallahassee really does have a homelessness issue. i really don't think it's safe for people to be sleeping outside with no protection or to be going to people's home to ask for money. there has to be a better alternative.
