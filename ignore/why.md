WORK IN PROGRESS!!!!

The first time I seriously thought about running for City Commission was in April 2021, when I was [passionately pleading](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r-MguFHynA8&t=8640s) with our Mayor and City Commissioners to extend the [utility moratorium](https://twitter.com/shelbyjgreen/status/1385062263241990152). 

My [advocacy](https://chng.it/XrZxFymJLc) was unsuccessful -- the City ended its utility moratorium, without public notice or a standardized recovery plan, despite debt relief options offered from both the [City](https://www.talgov.com/you/you-products-home-project-share.aspx) and [State](https://www.ourflorida.com). 

Despite the -____, my experience was insightful. I learned first-hand how our City works... and it's unacceptable. 

In a City with the [highest poverty rate in Florida](), and ______ being [killed by gun violence](), it is unacceptable for our local government to ignore calls for responsive policymaking, especially during a pandemic. 

The last time I went to a City Commission meeting was in [November 2021](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TomAmzgDnlM). to advocate for ... and really made me question, "Would they even care if I died on the road they're paid to maintain?"

Their lack of response to my demands for safety and protection solified my desire to fight for a better Tallahassee. 

I want to live in a Tallahassee where:
- residents' concerns are not silenced, ignored, or dismissed 
- no one worries about getting home safely
- our housing stock is actively preserved and upgraded, _____ (mold)
- we take advantage of our public utility to lead the charge on decarbonization and neighborhood-scale solar development and energy efficiency
- residents feel empowered, and safe enough, to reduce their reliance on natural gas, by using public transit or other forms of commuting
- buildings are designed to that require less electricity, and incorporating solar in building design
- students see Tallahassee so something more than a place where they get their degree. A place to launch ideas and start businesses

The world is changing, and becoming more unexpected. We need a government that acknowledges that reality through action, through data-informed, adaptive and responsive policymaking. 

We deserve a future where our lives are treated with respect and care, where re-occuring problems, like car crashes and traffic deaths, are addressed.</li>

We're recovering from a pandemic. Open to ideas and competition by increasing retention from graduating students at TCC, FAMU, and FSU. We have access to an abundance of research (FSU, FAMU, TCC) and social resources (non-profits) that we're letting go to waste.</li>

TLDR;
I'm running to prioritize the public in public funds, services, and infrastructure. Improve public/customer service and transparency (through open data/tech).
