had an interesting day today. 

woke up to the news that buffalo had essentially a massacre, fueled by racism. my immediate reaction was that i wasn't suprrised. i didn't think about it again until i went on twitter and saw the discourse. then i saw photos of the victims and thought about how devastiting it would be if i lost my mom in that way. just painful. 

i felt a lot of emotions, and they're starting to come back as i write, but i mainly felt anger and rage. a little of that was expressed through my tweets. i was tweeting because i am tired of hearing about the constant vicious attacks driven by unchecked racism. and how im tired of living in a society or culture where people are so woke online, but in real life are complicit to racism. just sit by as both subtle and apparent displays of racism occur. 

it's really left me questioning why the responsibility to combat racism, and other systems of oppression, is left to its victims. 

after my episode online, i read the ocoee riot recounting written by Zora Neale Hurston. i was then going to spiral into reading more stories about Black massacres but then Benji said 'let's go outside'. we went for a bike ride, which was such a good idea. it gave me a chance to clear my mind and re-focus my attention. 

it's so easy to become engulfed in rage and sadness and grief. 

it's necessary to have an outlet, and access to an outlet, to protect yourself. 

while biking, we ran into dara, who is another black woman who has had a similar experience as me in local political circles. she's moving to DC soon because she's tired of tallahassee. 

tallahassee is such a depressing city, when you live outside the few communities that are here. 

i was so high btw. i took an edible before the ride and definitely to fuck around and took a whole. i was so high i felt like i saw layers of earth and nature. such an experience. im proud i was able to control my thoughts, because there were moments when i could've spiralled, but didn't. im happy about that. 

on our way back from the park, we got caught in the rain. ended up locking up our bikes at a nearby bike rack then ubered home. there should be a dial-a-ride program that's similar to FSU's Safe Bus and has the capacity to accomodate bikes. that would've come in handy today. 

i think ive pretty much recounted my day. making time to dump events from the day is good. makes it easier to record, process, and prepare. 

until tomorrow!

oh -- im turning in my petitions tomorrow! should be nice. i'll make sure to get a proper photo. don't know how many we have, but i'm hoping it's a total of at least 1,000. we'll find out.
