date: '2022-05-10'

---

Sign up to:
1. **[Table](https://calendly.com/shelbytlh/tabling?month=2022-05)** -- set up a table and ask passersby to sign a petition to get me (Shelby) on the ballot
2. **[Canvass](https://calendly.com/shelbytlh/canvassing?month=2022-05)** -- walk up to people in a public space or knock on doors to ask them to sign a petition
3. **[Leaflet](https://calendly.com/shelbytlh/leafletting)** -- leave campaign literature and petitions on people's doorknobs
4. **[Collect filled-out petitions](https://calendly.com/shelbytlh/picking-up-petitions)** -- pick up petitions from those that filled one out via leafletting
