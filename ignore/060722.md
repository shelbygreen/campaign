i just got back from the screening with the police benevolent association. my immediate thought after i was done was "i could've done better" and then i started overanalyzing my performance. im so annoyed with myself. why do i always do this? why can't i just be happy with my performance???? with myself???

in a couple of hours, i'll be at the debate hosted by ALERT. i just need to remember to speak from my heart, to be unapologetically honest, and to give it my all. at the event, i'll mainly be speaking to the voters. so acknowledge their struggle, pinpoint problems & roadblocks, and offer solutions! i! can! do! this!

if i get the endorsement from PBA, ok. if not, it's ok too. 

alright, time to get ready and get into the zone.

--

9:15pm -- im back from the debate. overall, i think did well without prepping. but i could've done better if i adequately prepped for the debate. i need to make sure i'm ready for the next one. in the future, i can provide more well-rounded answers by taking my time to answer the question and return back to the theme: providing opportunity for financial stability by investing in public infrastructure and policy that serves the benefit of the public, not corporations, developers, or others.

i think adner did a good job. he was aggressive, which influenced me and made me more aggressive. i didn't want to be that aggressive. part of me feels a bit of guilt. like i didn't treat Cmr. Williams-Cox with respect. i should apologize for that. this is such a weird feeling. 

moving forward: treat people with respect, even if you don't like them. and prepare ahead of time & adequately!
