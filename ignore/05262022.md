i had my soil and water meeting today. it was pretty standard, nothing crazy happened. while i was in the meeting though, i was thinking about how simple local government can and should be. and strange enough, being in the meeting reminded me why i want to be city commissioner. i care about solving civic problems and helping the public.

i have a really good resume of solving problems and building tools. i need to highlight that when i make the case that i'm best the person for the job. 

-- 

i spoke to trish brown for a little bit today. it was nice talking to her. i asked her how ms. miasia was doing because i read (somewhere, now i cant find the source) that she was dropping out of the race. i talked to her about doing a small get-together with other black women who are in the politics, policy, organizing, and community building space. just to get to know one another better. and suggested that she share it with Ms. Miaisha to see if she'd be interested. we'll see what happens!

i also called Ms. Miaisha. she didn't answer, but im going to see if it's possible that i can get her support for my campaign. 

i went to the village square event tonight. i had a nice time at the event. i ran into reo and saw david bellamy. the conversation was good. i'm kind of tired so im not going to recount everything, but i had a great conversation with someone at my table and i was quick to share that i was running for city commission. i think i was confident and strong, and i loved that. i shared my opinions about where our local govt is lacking and what work still needs to be done.
