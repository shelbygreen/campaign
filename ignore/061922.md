8:30pm -- traveling and the campaign has finally caught up to me. im so tired. i'm about to watch sex and the city and just relax. it was nice having monet here to talk through politics and strategy, but it was a little bit too much. i started to overthink my strategy and feel like i needed to do everything at once. i need to remember to take my time and go at a pace that is sustainable for me now, that centers relationship building and infrastructure/network development. that'll pay off in the long-term. 

at the ben crump event, adner told me that some church was having a candidate forum and didn't extend the invites to other candidates. he found out through one of his supporters. and he was sharing information with me to encourage me to keep an eye out for that and to pressure the Pastor into extending future invitations to all candidates. 

when i told Monet, she questioned Adner's motives. because if Adner really wanted to encourage equal opportunity, he would've called me as soon as he found out. 

this is where politics and strategy really comes into play. questioning motives and if he's sharing information to anger or distract me. one thing i do know is adner and dianne will be fighting for the Black establishment base -- getting the people at the top. i will have to go for the people at the bottom. remember that, Shelby. fight for the people at the bottom, whose issues are ignored and dismissed because they do not have access to power or capital. 

while at the ben crump event, i met a student -- Andre Silver -- who was inspiring to talk to. we were on the same wavelength in terms of preserving and expanding on the legacy that our Black leaders left for us. it's incredible, and i have to keep that in mind. 

overall, today was successful but also draining! i'm glad i didn't let the politics distract me. it's good information to know, and something i need to at least be more aware of, but it's also mentally draining to run a million and one scenarios in your head. it's easy to become paranoid and anxious that way.
