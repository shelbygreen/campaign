5:35pm -- im getting closer to figuring out how i want to run and organize my campaign, which is such a satisfying feeling. to feel like im no longer unsteady. to feel solidifed in direction and strategy. it's nice. 

i wanted to jot down some notes from a very very brief canvassing session. i got a phone call from julianna, asking for yard signs. im so glad she called to remind me. it's so easy to lose track -- which is partly why im writing all this down, to make it easier to follow-up. anyway, she called, so i walked over to her house with some yard signs. she was really sweet when she spoke to me. she commended me on my campaign and said she appreciated me sharing my brightness with the world. she also said something that was interesting -- that i'm just expanding my sphere of protection. going from my family/friends, those impacted by city's decision to end the utility moratorium, and now all city residents. this really is such a natural progression, and i want to make sure people can connect those dots.

should i call out kristin dozier for passing out adner's yard signs w/ hers? maybe, maybe not. but is it ethical? is adner receiving an unearned advantage? is it anticompetitive? i don't know. but im done thinking about it for now. 

i actually went off the rails lol. after visiting julianna, i walked home and stopped by bonnie's house. she said she was just thinking about asking me for a yard sign. we talked for a bit and she was really passionate about public infrastructure -- she wants to see buses w/ simple and direct routes. she also said mailboxes should be placed as close to the road as possible. it seemed like she cared about traffic safety and congestion, too. and her talking gave me an idea for a map: traffic issues, like w/ cars parking on both sides of the street, making it more difficult to pass through. 

then i felt like something was tugging me, so i left and ran into _____.

8:02pm -- watching the debate (Melanin Mothers Meet)

8:39pm -- the debate just ended. i wish i would've gone to the event. i knew it was happening, and wanted to go, but ended up not prioritizing it because i didn't put it on my calendar and had too many other things to do/think about. it would've been a good networking opportunity. in the future, i need to make sure i add community events to my calendar and try my best to attend them. i even missed out on the neighborhood prep event and i definitely wanted to do to that. ugh, im still figuring out my working schedule, but definitely need to get it together.
