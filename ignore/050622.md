may 6, 2022

it was so weird being in tampa. it felt like i had independence. i didn't have to worry about the campaign or taking care of benji. my only focus was to learn, connect, share, and have fun. which is exactly what i did. but being home now feels different. 

i feel the weight again of my campaign and also of being a wife. i was less reliant on benji when i was in tampa. almost too independent. now, im reliant on him again. i asked him to run a campaign errand and show face at a community event. 

i dont want to be too reliant on benji, especially because i feel like like he can't rely on me. i don't clean, keep the home in order, or cook meals. but i used to. my excuse is that i've been too busy, but i also don't want to. why am i so willing to take care of perfect strangers instead of those closest to me? i feel so much guilt over this. of being a bad wife. and it makes me question if i got married too soon. should i have waited longer or thought deeply about it? i liked what benji provided -- safety, stability, and love. but what did i provide in return? at first, i was so willing to cook for benji and spend my time with him. now i'm less willing. 

i need to try to be a better wife. i've gotten better in the sex department, now i need to get better when it comes to household chores.
