today was kind of weird. and unexpected. it started with me just being too lazy about doing campaign work. i think i was feel overwhelmed but also still tired from driving/traveling and having trouble getting back into the swing of things. 

took a nap and when i got up, i saw i had a missed call. returned the call and it was gerri seay. she called me to tell me that ramon alexander isn't running for re-election and that i should consider running for his seat because i'd be a more "viable" candidate in that race. i just let her talk. i should've been more strong. that was my opportunity to show that im not patient. but next time i will. 

also, i was too slow to respond to someone and they said it felt like i had forgotten about them and they weren't interested in meeting anymore. i understand that. but it took me a while to accept it. i have to be more proactive and better at following through, and not being too ambitious, but i also need to recognize that i can only do my best. not everyone is going to be satisfied, and that's okay. it is what it is. 

i want to start blogging my campaign experience, but im still insecure about how people will respond to it. that's normal. at least im recording my thoughts/expriences and maybe i'll feel more comfortable sharing in the future.

today, monet reminded me that im going through growing pains. everything is going to be alright and work out, but i need to just trust the process and both adapt and learn as i go about this journey.
